B1 responsive proof boundary

The in-app Browser viewport capability was requested with width=390 and
height=844. The browser page still reported window.innerWidth=845,
window.innerHeight=998, and max-width:767px=false after the override and
reload. A fresh tab reported its fixed 1280x720 viewport as well.

The resulting fixed-viewport screenshots are retained as diagnostic attempts,
not accepted narrow-mobile proof. The source layout still contains the
responsive contract (AppShell max-md drawer and Course card md:grid-cols-2),
but this campaign does not claim browser-level narrow-mobile proof until the
in-app viewport capability is operational.
