## Unit 3: Cellular respiration

Learners should be able to:

- explain the major stages of cellular respiration;
- explain why oxygen is the terminal electron acceptor in aerobic respiration;
- compare aerobic respiration with fermentation.

The assessment contract uses short-answer questions with one concise exact
answer. Questions must be answerable from the supplied packet and must cite a
reachable source locator.
