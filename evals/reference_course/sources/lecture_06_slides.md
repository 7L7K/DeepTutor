# Lecture 6 slides — Cellular respiration

## Slide 2 — The route of carbon

Glucose → glycolysis → pyruvate → pyruvate oxidation → acetyl-CoA → citric
acid cycle → electron transport chain and oxidative phosphorylation.

## Slide 5 — Capturing energy

Glycolysis makes a small ATP return and NADH. The citric acid cycle makes
NADH and FADH2. These reduced carriers deliver electrons to the electron
transport chain.

## Slide 7 — The final acceptor

In aerobic respiration, oxygen accepts electrons at the end of the electron
transport chain. The reaction produces water and permits continued electron
flow.

## Slide 9 — Aerobic respiration and fermentation

Both pathways can begin with glycolysis. Aerobic respiration continues through
oxidative phosphorylation using oxygen; fermentation regenerates NAD+ so
glycolysis can continue when the aerobic electron-acceptor route is unavailable.
